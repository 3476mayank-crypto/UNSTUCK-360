Doubt: Find $\int x \cdot e^{x^2}\,dx$ and explain why $u$-substitution works here.
1

Spot the inner derivative: Let $u = x^2$, so $du = 2x\,dx \implies x\,dx = \frac{1}{2}\,du$.

2

Integrate & substitute back: $\frac{1}{2}\int e^u\,du = \frac{1}{2}e^{x^2} + C$.
